What kids learn

  1. The board and the pieces. Setup, and how every piece moves.
  2. Check, checkmate and stalemate. How a game is won, lost or drawn.
  3. Special moves and opening basics. Castling, en passant, promotion.
  4. Checkmate patterns and tactics. Back rank and ladder mates.
  5. Practice games and puzzles. Playing with a friend, then solving together.
  6. Mini tournament. Real games with real rules, plus certificates.
